How we
read a market

Six rules behind every number on this site. They exist so you can disagree with us: if you can see how a figure was built, you can judge whether it matters to you.

01

No single risk score

We never collapse a market into one grade. A score hides the one detail that would have changed your mind, so we list indicators individually instead.

02

Yield is split at the source

Every APY is broken into what the protocol earns and what an incentive program pays. The second part has an end date, and we show it.

03

Indicators are named, not ranked

“Single oracle feed” and “upgradeable by multisig” are facts. Which one is worse depends on your position size and horizon, not on our weighting.

04

Every figure traces to a source

TVL, utilisation and rates are read on-chain. Each one links back to the contract and block it came from, so you can verify it yourself.

05

We track change, not snapshots

A market that quietly doubled its incentive share last week is a different market. Indicators carry the date they changed state.

06

We say what we cannot see

Off-chain custody, unpublished audits and admin intent are not observable. We mark them as unknown rather than scoring them as safe.

Questions you
should be asking

WHY IS THERE NO LOW / HIGH RISK LABEL?+
Because a single label would be ours, not yours. A market with one oracle feed may be fine for a small position held for a week and unacceptable for a large one held for a year. We publish the indicators we found and let you weigh them against your own position.
WHAT IS ORGANIC APY?+
The part of the yield the protocol produces on its own — borrower interest, trading fees, real revenue. The rest is paid from an incentive program and stops when that program ends. If headline APY is 8.9% and organic APY is 3.1%, then 5.8% of your return has an expiry date.
WHERE DOES THE DATA COME FROM?+
Rates, TVL and utilisation are read directly from the protocol contracts on Arc. Audit status and admin configuration are compiled from published sources and labelled with the date we checked. Anything we cannot observe on-chain is marked unknown rather than assumed safe.
WHAT COUNTS AS A RISK EVENT?+
An indicator changing state — an incentive program announcing an end date, TVL concentration crossing a threshold, a contract being upgraded, or an oracle changing its source. We record the change and the block it happened in, not just the current value.
DO YOU TELL ME WHERE TO DEPOSIT?+
No. We do not rank markets, recommend allocations, or sell placement. The product's job is to make the trade-offs visible before you commit capital. The deposit decision stays with you, and so does the outcome.
